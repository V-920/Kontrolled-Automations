Frequently Asked Questions

Does this replace my sales team?

No. It handles repetitive follow-up so your team can spend more time on actual conversations.

Can it connect to my existing tools?

Yes. The workflow can be connected to forms, email, CRMs and APIs through n8n.

Can the messages be customized?

Yes. The AI prompt and workflow rules can be tailored to your business, offer and sales process.

What happens when a lead replies?

The production workflow can detect the reply and stop the automated sequence so a human can take over.
